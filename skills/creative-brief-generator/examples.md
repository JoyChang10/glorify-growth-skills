# Complete Example: Daily Prayer Habit Creative Brief

## Assumptions

- Goal: generate qualified pre-order interest.
- Audience: Christian women aged 25–45.
- Platform: one 9:16 master video for TikTok and Instagram Reels.
- Product details beyond “Glorify Ring” are not supplied; use the approved pre-order page for factual details.

## Creative concept

- **Concept name:** The Five-Minute Window
- **Core idea:** A creator in a parked car after a busy morning describes no longer waiting for an ideal prayer routine. The ring appears as a visible part of a small intentional pause, not as the cause of a promised result.
- **Why this could work:** The scene creates immediate recognition for people with crowded routines, then uses a creator-led, hook-first format before any product mention. It is built to feel like a genuine reflection rather than a polished ad.
- **SGE frameworks used:**
  - **UGC Open Clip with App Demo:** open with a familiar moment, then introduce the product context.
  - **Hook-Led Short-Form Video Framework:** use a clear first-second tension and one simple message.
  - **Demo Skit Hook Framework:** establish a relatable situation before showing the product.
  - **Small Creator Network Testing and Scaling:** test the hook with a small creator group before increasing production.

## Audience insight

- **Who this is for:** Christian women balancing work, caregiving, relationships, or a full home life who want their faith practice to feel possible in ordinary moments.
- **Emotion or problem:** Frustration with waiting for the “perfect” quiet moment and the desire for a more realistic, shame-free routine.

## Hook options

1. **Curiosity:** “The five minutes after drop-off became the part of my day I protect most.”
2. **Personal story:** “I kept telling myself I’d pray when life got quieter.”
3. **Identity:** “For women whose quiet time never looks like the Pinterest version.”
4. **Problem/solution:** “I don’t need a perfect morning routine. I need one intentional pause.”
5. **Question/contrast:** “What if your faith practice fit inside the life you already have?”

## Video structure

| Timing | Story beat | Spoken words | On-screen text | Visual / product moment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook | “I kept telling myself I’d pray when life got quieter.” | “Waiting for a perfect quiet time?” | Parked car; creator exhales, looks toward camera. |
| 3–10s | Emotional setup | “But most days, the house is busy, work starts early, and quiet never arrives on schedule.” | “Real life is loud.” | Short cuts: steering wheel, coffee, bag on passenger seat. |
| 10–22s | Reflection + product context | “So I started protecting one small pause instead. This is the moment I come back to what matters.” | “One small intentional pause.” | Approved close-up of Glorify Ring, then creator’s natural reflection. |
| 22–30s | CTA | “If you’re building a faith routine that fits real life, you can see Glorify Ring’s pre-order details in my bio.” | “See approved pre-order details” | Creator looks into camera; simple end card with approved brand treatment. |

## Creator script

“I kept telling myself I’d pray when life got quieter. [Pause. Creator is parked, natural light.] But most days, the house is busy, work starts early, and quiet never arrives on schedule. [Cut to coffee and bag on the passenger seat.] So I stopped waiting for the perfect routine and started protecting one small pause instead. [Show approved close-up of Glorify Ring.] This is just a simple cue for the moment I want to come back to what matters. If you’re trying to build a faith routine that fits real life, you can see Glorify Ring’s approved pre-order details in my bio.”

## Shot list

| Scene | Visual | Camera / edit note | Product moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Creator in parked car | Selfie camera; hold eye contact for first line. | None. |
| 2 | Coffee, bag, quiet car interior | Two 0.5–1 second B-roll cuts. | None. |
| 3 | Creator’s hand / ring close-up | Natural movement; use only approved product framing. | First product reveal. |
| 4 | Creator to camera | Slow, no dramatic transition; captions stay readable. | Optional subtle ring visibility. |
| 5 | Approved end card | 1–2 seconds. | Approved CTA only. |

## Creator direction

- **Personality and tone:** Warm, grounded, candid; speak like sharing something with a friend.
- **Filming style:** Casual 9:16, daylight, minimal makeup or production polish required, captions on.
- **Do:** Keep the creator’s own wording if the core idea remains intact; show a believable daily context; keep the ring reveal concise.
- **Avoid:** Driving while filming, performing a testimony, promising a spiritual change, treating prayer as a productivity metric, or using unapproved product facts.

## CTA options

- **Awareness:** “Follow along if you’re building a faith practice for real life.”
- **App download:** “See the approved Glorify app details through the link in bio.”
- **Pre-order:** “See Glorify Ring’s approved pre-order details in my bio.”
- **Purchase:** “Check current availability through the approved link in bio.”

## Testing variations

| Variation | Change | Why test it | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: Personal story | Use Hook 2 and the car-pool setting. | Tests emotional self-recognition. | Strong three-second hold and qualified clicks. |
| B: Identity | Use Hook 3 with a busy kitchen morning. | Tests audience identification. | Saves, shares, and qualified clicks. |
| C: Product discovery | Start with ring close-up at second 1, then context. | Tests whether faster product clarity reduces or improves drop-off. | Hold rate plus landing-page conversion. |
| D: Desk reset | Use a cluttered desk instead of the car. | Tests a workday context for the same message. | Completion rate and clicks. |
| E: FAQ response | Start: “Is this only for morning prayer?” | Tests practical-intent framing. | Comments and qualified clicks. |
