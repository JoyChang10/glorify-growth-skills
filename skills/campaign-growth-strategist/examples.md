# Example: Pre-order Creative Test

## Input

**Goal:** Generate Glorify Ring pre-orders on paid Instagram Reels over 14 days.

| Creative | Hook / format | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Pre-orders | Revenue |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | Product-first ring demo | $300 | 30,000 | 210 | 3 | $450 |
| B | Creator's busy-day prayer story | $300 | 28,000 | 280 | 8 | $1,200 |

**Assumptions:** Same audience, placement, landing page, product price, and attribution window. No watch-time or retention data is available.

## Performance summary

Creative B has stronger click-through and more attributed pre-orders at equal spend. **Observation:** B generated 280 clicks (1.0% CTR) and 8 pre-orders; A generated 210 clicks (0.7% CTR) and 3 pre-orders. **Hypothesis:** the creator-led, relatable story improves both qualified attention and product relevance. Confidence is moderate because the sample is small and only two creatives were tested.

## Key learnings

1. **Observation:** B's CPA is $37.50 versus A's $100.00, assuming pre-orders are the conversion event. **Hypothesis:** starting with a relatable faith-practice tension is more motivating than starting with a product demonstration.
2. **Observation:** B's click-to-pre-order conversion is 2.9% versus A's 1.4%. **Hypothesis:** B better prepares viewers for what they will find after the click; this is not proof that the landing page is fully effective.

## Next experiments

| Experiment | Hypothesis | Single primary change | Success rule | Guardrail metric | Measurement plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1: faster product reveal | Reveal the ring at second 5 instead of after the story setup. | Reveal timing only | Maintain or improve pre-order CPA against B | CTR does not fall materially | Same audience and spend; optimize to pre-order |
| B2: identity hook | A Christian-women identity hook will retain qualified viewers better than the busy-day hook. | Opening line only | Improve CTR or pre-order CPA versus B | Click-to-pre-order CVR | Same creator, visual sequence, CTA, and landing page |
| B3: creator replication | The story pattern can transfer to a second creator. | Creator only | CPA stays within an agreed range of B | Comment quality and CVR | Equal test budget and audience |

## Scale, iterate, stop

| Creative / campaign | Decision | Why | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: product-first demo | Iterate | It generated some clicks but weak pre-order efficiency; one test cannot justify stopping the entire product-demo format. | Rebuild with a viewer-first tension before the demo. |
| B: busy-day story | Scale cautiously | It leads on CTR, pre-orders, and CPA in this matched test. | Increase budget gradually while running B1 and B2; monitor CPA and conversion rate. |

The relevant retrieved SGE hook, creator-UGC, and conversion frameworks should explain the choice of story-first variants. They are strategic evidence, not a promise that B will scale indefinitely.
