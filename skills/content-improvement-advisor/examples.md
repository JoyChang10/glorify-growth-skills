# Example 1: Uploaded TikTok Script — Improve a Weak Prayer-Habit Ad

## Input creative

**Platform:** TikTok/Reels

**Goal:** Pre-orders

**Audience:** Christians in the US who want to pray more consistently

**Draft script:** “Meet Glorify Ring, the smart Christian ring that helps you pray every day. It is designed to help you build better habits. Pre-order yours today.”

**Assumptions:** No performance data or approved feature details beyond the prayer-habit positioning are available.

# Content Improvement Review

## Summary

The draft is clear about the product category but gives viewers no personal reason to care before the sales message. It leads with a feature claim, has no recognizable daily-life moment, and asks for a pre-order before establishing value. **SGE-backed recommendation:** lead with a relatable tension and introduce the product as a natural part of the resolution. **Creative suggestion:** use a creator’s real morning or workday transition rather than a generic tech setup.

## Problems identified

1. **Priority 1 — no viewer-first hook:** The opening talks about Glorify before it talks about the viewer’s life.
2. **Priority 2 — no emotional journey:** The script names the problem but never makes it felt through a moment or story.
3. **Priority 3 — CTA is too early:** Asking for a pre-order before showing context creates unnecessary friction.

## Recommended improvements

1. **SGE-backed recommendation:** Open on a real tension—wanting to pray more while life stays busy—then reveal Glorify Ring after the audience recognizes themselves. This uses the hook-first, creator-context pattern found in the retrieved UGC and emotional-storytelling frameworks.
2. **SGE-backed recommendation:** Keep the product moment short and visual. It should clarify the creator’s small pause, not become a feature list.
3. **Creative suggestion:** Use a parked car, morning kitchen, or work-desk transition so the prayer-habit challenge has a believable setting.

## Revised creative direction

**The five-minute window:** A creator says she used to wait for life to become quieter before praying. After a recognizable busy-life montage, she protects one small intentional moment; Glorify Ring appears as a cue within that moment. The intended reaction is “that is my life,” followed by “I want to learn more.”

## Improved hooks

1. **Personal story:** “I kept telling myself I’d pray when life got quieter.”
2. **Identity:** “For anyone whose quiet time never looks like the Pinterest version.”
3. **Problem/solution:** “I don’t need a perfect prayer routine. I need one intentional pause.”
4. **Question:** “What if spending more time with God didn’t require finding more time?”

## Improved script and structure

| Timing | Spoken words | On-screen text | Visual / product moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | “I kept telling myself I’d pray when life got quieter.” | Waiting for life to slow down? | Creator in a parked car or kitchen, looking to camera. |
| 3–10s | “But most days, there’s work, people needing me, and a hundred things to do.” | Real life is loud. | Quick real-life B-roll. |
| 10–22s | “So I stopped waiting for the perfect routine and started protecting one small pause to come back to God.” | One small intentional pause. | Approved Glorify Ring close-up after the setup. |
| 22–32s | “Glorify Ring is part of that small moment for me. If you’re building a prayer habit that fits real life, see the approved pre-order details in my bio.” | See approved pre-order details | Creator to camera, then approved end card. |

## Creator direction

- **Tone and personality:** Warm, reflective, plain-spoken, and personal.
- **Filming and edit style:** Casual 9:16, natural lighting, captions, a few quick B-roll cuts.
- **Do:** Use a real routine and adapt the wording naturally.
- **Avoid:** Claims that the ring guarantees consistency or spiritual change; polished technology-commercial visuals; fake urgency.

## CTA options

- **Awareness:** “Follow along if you’re building a faith practice for real life.”
- **App download:** “See the approved Glorify app details in my bio.”
- **Pre-order:** “See approved Glorify Ring pre-order details in my bio.”
- **Purchase:** “Check current availability through the approved link in bio.”

# Example 2: Uploaded Reel Screenshots — Analyze Visual Storytelling

**User input:** Three screenshots from a 25-second Instagram Reel: (1) a creator staring at a crowded to-do list with the text "I keep meaning to pray," (2) a close-up of her hand and Glorify Ring, and (3) a product card reading "Pre-order now." No transcript is supplied.

**Approach:** State that the analysis is based on screenshots rather than motion, pacing, or audio. Extract the visual progression: relatable tension, product reveal, then CTA. Review text hierarchy, expression, setting, ring visibility, and whether the message is understandable without sound. Do not score voiceover, sound design, retention edits, or exact timing as if they were observed.

**Useful improvement:** Keep the first frame viewer-first, add a middle screenshot or visual beat that shows the creator choosing a quiet moment, then use the ring close-up as the resolution. Test a personal, spoken end card against the static product card.
