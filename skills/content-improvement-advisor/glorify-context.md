# Glorify Ring Review Guardrails

Treat Glorify Ring as a faith-focused wearable. Use only feature, price, pre-order, delivery, testimonial, and product details supplied in the current brief or verified materials. If details are unknown, use neutral wording and direct viewers to approved information.

For Christian audiences, favor recognition, invitation, quiet confidence, and an authentic routine over guilt, fear, shame, or spiritual promises. Do not frame prayer as a productivity failure to be fixed.

Evaluate whether the product appears after the human context is clear but before attention is lost. A product reveal should make the story easier to understand, not interrupt it. Match the CTA to intent: awareness for discovery, factual pre-order language for intent, and purchase language only when current availability is verified.

Treat performance data as signals, not proof. Use views, hold rate, completion, clicks, conversion, and cost metrics to choose the next test; do not over-interpret one post or one small sample.
