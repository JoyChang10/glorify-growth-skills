# Complete Example: Glorify Ring Pre-order Plan

## Assumptions

- Glorify Ring’s exact features, price, and launch date are not supplied; use “faith-focused wearable” and route clicks to the approved pre-order page.
- One creator can film several concepts in a single session; five videos per week means 20 posts over four weeks.

## Campaign objective

Increase qualified pre-order traffic and pre-orders from Christian women aged 25–45 on TikTok and Instagram over four weeks. Primary metric: pre-orders. Diagnostic metrics: qualified clicks, landing-page conversion, three-second hold rate, and completion rate.

## Audience insight

Many Christian women want a faith practice that fits an already full day, but content that feels overly polished, preachy, or product-first can feel inauthentic. A small, visible ritual cue can be more relatable than a broad promise of transformation.

## Strategic hypothesis

If creators show a specific real-life moment that interrupts or supports an existing faith routine, then qualified clicks and pre-orders will improve because the product is framed in familiar context before the pre-order ask.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Job | Core message | Primary format | Evidence/principle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Everyday faith moments | Recognition | Faith can have a place in an ordinary busy day. | Creator POV / mini-vlog | Relatable creator-led short-form storytelling |
| Ritual cue | Product understanding | A small cue can make an intended practice easier to remember. | Close-up demo with narration | Clear hook plus simple product demonstration |
| Shared reflection | Trust | People use faith practices differently; creators show their own. | Direct-to-camera + B-roll | Authentic creator UGC |
| Pre-order clarity | Conversion | Know what the next action is and why to take it now. | Creator recommendation / FAQ | Visible CTA with clear brand capture |

## Recommended formats

- **Creator POV mini-vlog:** 12–20 seconds; use a real moment, one thought, and a natural product reveal.
- **Close-up demo:** 8–15 seconds; show one interaction only, with readable on-screen text.
- **Answer-to-comment:** 15–25 seconds; use an authentic question or approved FAQ, not manufactured social proof.
- **Three-scene slideshow/video:** situation → reflection → pre-order action; reserve for message-led posts.

## Video concepts

1. **The morning that starts before I’m ready** — creator gets children/household/day started, pauses for one quiet beat, then shows Glorify Ring.
   - Hook A: “My mornings used to start before I had a second to breathe.”
   - Hook B: “The smallest part of my morning routine is the one I protect most.”
   - Hook C: “For the women whose quiet time never looks like the Pinterest version.”
   - CTA: “See the Glorify Ring pre-order details through the link in bio.”
   - Creator/production note: film natural daylight and avoid staging a perfect routine.

2. **The car-pool reset** — creator speaks from a parked car after drop-off; one sentence on returning to intention.
   - Hook A: “The five minutes after school drop-off are mine again.”
   - Hook B: “I stopped waiting for a perfect quiet time.”
   - Hook C: “A faith routine for the season when every room is loud.”
   - CTA: “Join the Glorify Ring pre-order list.”
   - Creator/production note: shoot parked only; no driving footage.

3. **One visible cue** — close-up of a routine transition (coffee, desk, walk) followed by the ring and a concise explanation.
   - Hook A: “This is my cue to pause before I open another tab.”
   - Hook B: “Not another productivity hack—just a better reminder.”
   - Hook C: “The tiny habit that helps me come back to what matters.”
   - CTA: “Explore Glorify Ring before pre-orders close.”
   - Creator/production note: show the actual moment first; product appears after the hook.

4. **What I wanted from a faith wearable** — direct-to-camera wish list, then clearly label which claims are confirmed.
   - Hook A: “I didn’t want a wearable that made faith feel like another task.”
   - Hook B: “Three things I care about before I pre-order any faith tech.”
   - Hook C: “If you’re considering a faith wearable, ask this first.”
   - CTA: “Review the confirmed Glorify Ring details on the pre-order page.”
   - Creator/production note: use only approved feature facts.

5. **The honest desk reset** — creator tidies a crowded desk and frames a small pause as a reset, not a cure-all.
   - Hook A: “My desk looks like this when my day gets away from me.”
   - Hook B: “I can’t make the to-do list disappear, but I can reset.”
   - Hook C: “For the afternoon slump that needs more than another coffee.”
   - CTA: “Tap to learn about pre-ordering Glorify Ring.”
   - Creator/production note: preserve the lived-in, imperfect setting.

6. **Comment / FAQ: ‘Is this only for morning prayer?’** — creator answers with approved product context and personal use.
   - Hook A: “Someone asked if this is only for mornings.”
   - Hook B: “My faith practice changes by the day, so my reminder has to as well.”
   - Hook C: “The question I had before I considered a faith wearable.”
   - CTA: “Find the full pre-order FAQ through the link in bio.”
   - Creator/production note: use a real comment or say “a common question.”

7. **Two versions of the same day** — split screen: rushing without a pause vs. intentionally taking a small moment; no exaggerated outcome claim.
   - Hook A: “Same schedule, different intention.”
   - Hook B: “I don’t need more hours. I need one intentional moment.”
   - Hook C: “The difference a small pause can make to my day.”
   - CTA: “Pre-order Glorify Ring to learn more.”
   - Creator/production note: keep both sides realistic; avoid implying dramatic life change.

8. **A gift with meaning** — creator considers a thoughtful gift for a Christian friend or family member, subject to approved positioning.
   - Hook A: “I wanted a meaningful gift that wouldn’t sit in a drawer.”
   - Hook B: “For the friend who already has every devotional journal.”
   - Hook C: “A gift idea for someone building a quieter routine.”
   - CTA: “Check Glorify Ring pre-order availability.”
   - Creator/production note: only use gift language if product and fulfillment timing support it.

9. **Creator reflection: what I return to** — a creator shares one brief personal reflection, then an unobtrusive product association.
   - Hook A: “The thought I come back to when my day feels scattered.”
   - Hook B: “This is the reminder I needed this week.”
   - Hook C: “Faith practices don’t have to be long to be meaningful.”
   - CTA: “If this resonates, see Glorify Ring’s pre-order page.”
   - Creator/production note: creator owns the reflection; no scripted testimony claims.

10. **Pre-order FAQ carousel** — three fast answers: who it is for, what is confirmed, and what happens after pre-order.
   - Hook A: “Before you pre-order a Glorify Ring, here’s what to know.”
   - Hook B: “Three pre-order questions answered in 15 seconds.”
   - Hook C: “The no-fluff Glorify Ring FAQ.”
   - CTA: “Read the full terms and pre-order details at the link in bio.”
   - Creator/production note: use current approved factual copy and captions.

## Creator and production guidance

Brief creators to use a true moment in their own routine, speak in their normal language, and lead with the situation before showing the product. Film 9:16 vertical video, 12–25 seconds, captions on, simple natural lighting, and one discernible idea per post. Shoot concepts 1, 3, 5, and 7 in one home session; concepts 2, 6, and 9 in a second session; use approved information only for concepts 4, 8, and 10.

## Testing matrix

| Variable | Variants | Hold constant | Decision metric | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook framing | busy-day tension / practical question / personal reflection | creator, format, CTA | 3-second hold rate | Take the top two framings into Week 2 if they clear the account median. |
| Format | POV mini-vlog / close-up demo | message, creator, length range | qualified click rate | Use the strongest format for conversion-led variants. |
| Product reveal | second 1–2 / after context | hook and CTA | hold rate + qualified clicks | Prefer the reveal point that preserves attention without reducing clicks. |
| CTA | “pre-order details” / “join pre-order list” / “read FAQ” | creative body | landing-page conversion | Keep the CTA that drives the highest qualified conversion, not just clicks. |

## SGE frameworks used

- **Hook-Driven Viral Video Format:** informs concise, curiosity- or tension-led openings.
- **Authentic Creator/UGC Content:** informs creator-owned, routine-based execution rather than polished ads.
- **Simple Product Demonstration:** informs one clear product interaction after context.
- **Multi-variant content testing:** informs controlled hook, format, reveal, and CTA iterations.
