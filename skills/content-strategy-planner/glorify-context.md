# Glorify Ring Context

Use this file as a planning guardrail, not a source of unverified product claims.

## Product framing

Treat Glorify Ring as a faith-focused wearable and companion-app product. Use only product features, pricing, launch timing, inventory details, testimonials, or claims supplied in the current brief or verified project materials. If those details are absent, write neutral language such as “Glorify Ring” or “a faith-focused wearable” and mark a clear assumption.

## Audience and creative principles

- Build from a real audience tension, ritual, identity, or moment instead of generic Christian imagery.
- Make faith representation respectful and voluntary; avoid guilt, fear, or promises about spiritual, health, or life outcomes.
- Use creator voice, everyday routines, visual proof, and clear context before any product mention.
- Adapt pacing and presentation to the platform while keeping one consistent campaign message.

## Decision rules

For pre-orders, make the value proposition and next action clear, but avoid fake scarcity. For awareness, prioritize an audience-recognizable opening and retention. For learning, test one major creative variable at a time before scaling a direction.

Use SGE examples for mechanisms—such as creator authenticity, hook clarity, UGC demos, and format iteration—not as permission to reuse another brand’s exact copy, footage, or claims.
