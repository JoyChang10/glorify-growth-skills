# Glorify Ring Creative Guardrails

Treat Glorify Ring as a faith-focused wearable. Do not assume or invent its features, app behavior, price, pre-order dates, stock, delivery, endorsements, or results. Use only facts in the user brief or verified project materials. When facts are unavailable, use neutral phrases such as “Glorify Ring,” “a faith-focused wearable,” and “see approved pre-order details.”

Build around a real moment, routine, tension, identity, or reflection. Represent faith respectfully and voluntarily. Avoid fear, shame, guilt, miracle language, medical claims, and a sales-first tone.

Lead with creator context before a product reveal. Show one product moment clearly only when it is supported by approved creative assets. Use direct, factual CTAs for pre-orders, downloads, purchases, or awareness; never manufacture urgency or comments.
